# Job Posting Skills Analysis # 

## Posting 1: Data Scientist

**Seniority:** Entry level

**Industry:** Energy / industrial technology company

### Required

- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field
- Foundational statistics, probability, experimental design, and machine learning concepts
- Python or R (coursework, research, internships, or independent projects)
- Data analysis and visualization tools: pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, SQL, Jupyter, matplotlib, seaborn, Power BI, or equivalents
- Explaining technical work clearly to technical and non-technical audiences
- Problem-solving, collaboration, attention to detail, willingness to learn

### Preferred

- Internship, research, capstone, or portfolio experience applying analytics or machine learning to a real problem
- Exposure to cloud data platforms, distributed computing, data pipelines, Git, containers, or ML lifecycle tools
- Time-series, simulation, optimization, computer vision, NLP, or generative AI
- Interest in renewable energy, physical systems, manufacturing, or engineering
- Validating results with subject-matter experts

### Hidden skills

- Version control, code review, testing, and reproducible workflows
- Documenting assumptions and data-quality issues
- Data governance and cybersecurity

## Posting 2: Senior Research and Policy Analyst

**Seniority:** Senior

**Industry:** Nonprofit environmental law organization

### Required

- Graduate degree in a related field or equivalent experience
- 3+ years of relevant experience
- Experience analyzing scientific and technical documents and presenting science and policy issues to experts and public audiences
- Strong research and organizational skills, attention to detail, independent work, and meeting deadlines
- Excellent writing, oral communication, and computer skills, including data analysis (Word, Excel, Adobe, Outlook, PowerPoint, Westlaw/LexisNexis, GIS)
- Awareness of diverse cultures and backgrounds; commitment to an inclusive work culture
- Willingness to register as a state lobbyist if required, with comfort tracking and complying with advocacy rules
- Willingness to travel as needed

### Preferred

- Experience with scientific or technical issues in land use, the electric sector, climate, energy, clean air policy, or environmental justice

### Hidden skills

- Drafting and managing letters, reports, and regulatory submissions
- Helping design and manage expert reports and testimony
- Preparing comments, attending hearings, and delivering testimony
- Community outreach and coalition building
- Project management and supervising interns

## Skills in Both Postings

- Data analysis
- Explaining technical or complex information to non-technical audiences
- Designing research and data-gathering approaches
- Problem-solving and attention to detail
- Collaborating with stakeholders across teams and organizations
- Strong written communication

## What Stood Out

What stood out to me were the vast required qualifications needed for the Senior position, from scientific analysis to managing interns. 
I've always thought of jobs as two-dimensional, meaning you had a specific task on a team and only did that one task. 
Additionally, I was surprised by the number of different programs and tools that these positions expect employees to understand and use. 
I wonder if all those tools are used on a daily basis or more infrequently?
